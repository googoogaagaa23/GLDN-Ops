# GLDN Ops v3.12.61

Date: 2026-10-19

## Prime Prices And Premium Store Defaults

Offer Review now separates Amazon's native Prime Member Price and Regular Price buying options instead of merging their primary prices. It uses the operator's configured Prime-member assumption. Prices from other sizes, subscriptions, used offers, unit-price labels, list prices and recommendations remain excluded. Conflicting prices within the chosen buying option and changed ASINs/variations still stop the read.

The reported clock layout is covered with $21.99 Prime, $26.95 regular and $28.99 for a different size. These are screenshot/fixture values, not a promise about a later live price. The selected exact-ASIN buying option supplies the price.

Preliminary estimates now default to Premium Store fees per operator preference, with the default shown in the estimate. Saved Premium, Anchor and Enterprise settings are retained; manual plan/category choices remain editable. Category exceptions, advertising, tax, shipping and missing expenses remain explicit.

Amazon evidence and the profit estimate show the price basis. A Join Prime prompt on the reader is surfaced as a warning to confirm the purchasing profile has Prime. Configured membership is an assumption, not verified account eligibility. No membership enrollment, buying-option selection, cart action, offer response or purchase is automated.
