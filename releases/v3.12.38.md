# GLDN Ops v3.12.38

## Changes
- Restores bulk selection and approval-gated ending for selected Block and Review rows, up to 200 exact listings per batch. No-match rows are excluded.
- Uses normal signed-in eBay pages, not private ending endpoints. Exact item IDs, selected counts and seller identity are verified before ending.
- One-use approvals survive reloads. Uncertain results never count as success or retry automatically. Check eBay Result performs read-only reconciliation.
- Refresh Policy Checks reclassifies saved rows without rereading the store or resetting their scan date.
- Narrows broad compatibility, warranty, OEM, AAA-quality and generic inspiration heuristics. Specific IP risks, pesticides and all aerosol spray cans remain flagged.
- Product Hunter v0.3.3 shares the calibrated 618-rule pack.

## Update
