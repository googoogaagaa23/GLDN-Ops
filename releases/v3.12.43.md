# GLDN Ops v3.12.43

## Stronger Pesticide Prevention

Update & Reload in each GLDN profile. Reopen Listing Policy Check and rerun pasted links. For saved Existing Listings Policy Audit results, click **Refresh Policy Checks**. Start a fresh complete scan to include newly posted listings.

The 639-rule pack includes 22 targeted pesticide no-list rules. It blocks botanical plant sprays (including neem/peppermint leaf-shine or nutrient sprays), rooting gels/powders/hormones, septic chlorine, pool/spa treatment chemicals, repellents and baits, disinfectants, and pesticidal or microbial kill claims. Common plurals, spelling variants and hyphenated forms are handled. Product Hunter v0.3.4 ships the same rules.

These are GLDN operator exclusions, including EPA-registered and natural versions, not a claim that eBay universally prohibits every pesticide. Ordinary tools, empty bottles, nutrition-only fertilizers and unclaimed cosmetic oils are not pesticide signals alone. Existing pesticide/spray-can blocks and private shared removal history remain in place.

Blocked links cannot be copied into the bulk lister through GLDN. Review existing flagged rows, select the intended batch and approve its exact End action. No listings are ended automatically.

Product Hunter also rechecks saved Ready results at copy time. Older hunts cannot export a newly blocked pesticide under the new policy version. If saved results are excluded, start a new hunt to refresh the displayed results.

## Verification

Regression checks cover observed removal families, keyword variants, negative controls, all product evidence fields, Ready-copy exclusion, Product Hunter search/detail decisions and existing-store classification. Isolated browser fixtures verify the real preflight screen and audit display; they are not a live end-to-end run on every computer.

Text screening cannot detect undisclosed ingredients or text that was not read from a product image. Use the checked output for every listing batch; posting directly through another lister bypasses GLDN screening.

Sources: [eBay pesticides policy](https://www.ebay.com/help/policies/prohibited-restricted-items/fertilizer-pesticides-policy?id=4630), [EPA pesticide definition](https://www.epa.gov/minimum-risk-pesticides/what-pesticide), [EPA minimum-risk conditions](https://www.epa.gov/minimum-risk-pesticides/conditions-minimum-risk-pesticides).
