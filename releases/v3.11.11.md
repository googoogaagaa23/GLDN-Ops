# GLDN Ops v3.11.11

## Scope

- Keep the eBay floating panel hidden during ordinary browsing.
- Restore a dependable way to open daily actions without relying on Chrome's protected popup page.
- Preserve every existing marketplace approval boundary.

## Daily Actions Shortcut

- Press `Ctrl+Shift+G` on the active signed-in eBay page to open the GLDN Ops daily-actions panel.
- The service worker sends one trusted extension message to that exact active tab.
- The shortcut does nothing when the active tab is not an eBay page.
- Opening the panel does not start a workflow or change marketplace data.

## Marketplace Safety

- Mark as Shipped still stops before activation and again before eBay Continue when shown.
- Order Note still stops before eBay Save.
- Move `.99` and reverse cleanup still stop before every eBay Submit.
- Seller metrics, sales snapshots, and listing limits still require review before dashboard or Tasks sync.
