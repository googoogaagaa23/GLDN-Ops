# GLDN Product Hunter v0.3.4

Uses the same 639-rule pack and stronger pesticide matching as GLDN Ops v3.12.43. Natural plant sprays, rooting products, septic/pool chemicals, repellents and microbial kill claims are excluded from Ready output. Common plurals, hyphens and spelling variants no longer bypass the pesticide check.

Pesticide and aerosol exclusions cannot be disabled by ordinary hunt filters. This is text-based screening, not eBay approval or a guarantee against every policy violation.

Saved Ready results are rechecked at copy time against the current policy pack. Newly blocked items are excluded, even if an older hunt had marked them Ready.

## Verification

Targeted tests cover pesticide exclusion in both search and detail phases, unchanged ordinary product handling and main-extension policy pack parity. No live listings are submitted.
