# GLDN Ops v3.12.50

Released 2026-10-06.

## Price Shortcut

Open the intended eBay Order Details, listing or native Revise page and choose GLDN Price +$0.10. The helper reads the current Item price and prepares exactly ten cents more. For example, 27.19 becomes 27.29; 27.99 becomes 28.09. It does not use the old order price or eBay's recommended price.

One listing is prepared at a time. Auctions, active variations, ambiguous inputs, changed prices, expired sessions and unverifiable redirects stop preparation. Retries reuse the original target instead of adding again. Only Item price is edited; final eBay Revise it is manual.

## Update

Use Update & Reload, then refresh open eBay tabs. Choose Daily > Price +$0.10 or the matching floating panel button. Check the old/new amounts and submit the native revision yourself.
