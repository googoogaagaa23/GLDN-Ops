## Offer Review

Open GLDN Offer Review on one pending native eBay Offer Received page, or use Daily > Offer Review. It opens read-only background tabs in the same Chrome profile for the exact listing SKU, matched Amazon product, and buyer feedback left for others. No other extension's review or purchase control is invoked.

The counteroffer is operator-chosen. Estimated profit uses dated official US fee presets and explicit Store/category settings, Amazon cost, taxes, shipping, ads, per-order fee, optional surcharges and confirmed Top Rated Plus discount. Missing costs are disclosed rather than silently treated as known. Category exceptions need a confirmed custom rate/per-order fee. Predictions are not guaranteed payouts.

Feedback counts include every page, reconcile native IDs and displayed ranges/totals, preserve identical comments from distinct transactions, and show Complete or Partial. Positive, neutral and negative percentages use all scanned entries. Non-positive comments are displayed without labeling a buyer dishonest or automatically rejecting them.

## Boundaries

No accept, decline, counteroffer submission, buyer block, purchase, address change or listing edit is automated. Copying an amount and returning to eBay leaves submission manual. Readers stop on identity changes, missing/ambiguous data, verification, skipped/repeated pages or changed totals. Paused feedback scans retain partial evidence; restarting scans from the beginning.
