# GLDN Ops v3.12.30

Released: 2026-08-31

## Paste-first Listing Policy Check

- Opens directly without requiring copied links.
- Accepts pasted Amazon links, product titles, ASIN-with-title rows, and CSV rows.
- Reads raw Amazon links sequentially in one inactive tab and caches completed products for safe resume.
- Removes the visible 500-word grid, official-policy cards, Discord cards, and Telegram cards.
- Removes the generic-brand and five-attestation gates. A brand name alone does not stop a product.
- Copies only readable no-match Ready links; Review and Blocked rows stay excluded.

## Policy decisions

- The shared pack has 580 rules: 576 official eBay rules, 2 GLDN operator Blocks, and 2 Discord Review warnings.
- Every pesticide product and every spray can is Blocked under explicit GLDN no-list rules.
- Conditional restricted-item matches remain Needs review.
- Community evidence remains Review-only.
- Invalid or stale policy data fails closed to Needs review.

## Existing listings

- Existing Listings Policy Audit remains read-only and exposes no selection, revision, relisting, or End control.
- It applies the same keyword rules to active listing titles and exports source-linked results.

## Verification

- Focused Listing Policy Check, Product Hunter, existing-listing audit, popup, and package contracts pass.
- The complete JavaScript test suite passes with the keyword-only policy behavior enabled.
- Public staging must additionally pass extension parsing, package safety, installer, updater, rollback, checksum, and manifest verification before publication.

## Boundary

- A Ready result means no published item-policy keyword matched the product words GLDN could read. It is not eBay approval.
- This workflow does not create, edit, end, relist, or submit an eBay listing.
