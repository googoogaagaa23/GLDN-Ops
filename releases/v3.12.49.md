# GLDN Ops v3.12.49

Released 2026-10-05.

## Unread Customer Messages

Open the GLDN icon > Daily > Unread Customer Messages, or use the floating eBay panel button.

Scan the native Unread from members folder, including paginated and automatically loaded rows. The complete view shows every found conversation, with pending, ignored and opened counts. Ignore/Restore stays local to the Chrome profile and eBay account. Changed previews return to pending; a relative clock change does not undo an ignore.

Open All Pending opens one eBay tab per unignored unread conversation. Exact account, buyer and conversation evidence are checked before opening. Opening can mark messages read on eBay. No replies, deletion or archiving are performed.

## Update

Use Update & Reload, then refresh open eBay tabs. Existing settings and other workflows are retained. Messages and Ignore preferences are not published to GitHub or sent to the shared dashboard.
