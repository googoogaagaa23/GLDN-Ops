## eBay Profit Audit

- Renames the review surface so the result is unmistakable: **Profit from saved notes**.
- Replaces misleading `$0.00` values with **Pending** until at least one reviewed order has confirmed saved-note earnings and Amazon cost.
- Shows exact coverage as `confirmed orders of reviewed orders`; a dollar result is labeled partial until every reviewed order is covered.
- Replaces the repeated Read 1 / Read 2 labels with **Saved-note profit** and **Independent Amazon profit**.
- Adds **Read All History**, which uses eBay's available Custom order-history range and preserves each order's calendar month during dashboard sync.
- Keeps one-month reads available for smaller, faster audits.

- The audit remains read-only and never purchases, cancels, refunds, ships, edits, or deletes an order.
