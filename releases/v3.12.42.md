# GLDN Ops v3.12.42

## Removed Listing History

Update & Reload, then open **Removed Listing History** in GLDN Ops.
Choose **Scan This eBay Account** in each signed-in eBay profile.

The scanner reads **Listings > Inactive > View Listings**, records the exact listing notice and reads available case explanations. It does not click Resolve actions beyond opening the case page, appeal, relist, revise, or end listings.

Incidents are shared privately through the existing authenticated dashboard. Other updated profiles use the combined history in Listing Policy Check and Existing Listings Policy Audit. Exact ASIN matches block; close product-title matches require review. Missing explanations stay unknown, and broad brand/category bans are not generated.

Wait for **Shared history verified**. A failed sync is not a completed cross-computer update. Use **Refresh Policy Checks** in a saved existing-listings audit after new history is shared.

## Verification

Automated checks cover multi-page reading, case identity, paired notices, duplicate merging, shared readback, offline-save handling, cross-profile checks, HTML escaping and desktop/mobile layout. The existing dashboard backend is deployed; 60 live incidents (56 ASINs) from one account were saved and read back exactly. Three detailed case explanations were read; the rest retain their exact listing notices. Not every account or workstation has been scanned.

The All-rows view, Block + Review selection, exact batch approval and uncertain-result recovery are retained. No marketplace listing was changed during release work.
