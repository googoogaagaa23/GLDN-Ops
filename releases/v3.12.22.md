# GLDN Ops v3.12.22

Released: 2026-08-22

## Cross-profile order-placement audit

- A completed Monthly eBay Profit month can seed exact eBay order units with order number, date, status, ASIN, quantity, and ship-to evidence.
- Each signed-in Amazon Chrome profile scans only its own order history and contributes exact matching order-item units to one shared computer/account/month audit.
- The audit deduplicates an Amazon order visible from more than one Chrome profile.
- Unit allocation distinguishes covered demand, same-recipient duplicates, possible different-recipient extras, purchases tied to canceled eBay orders, and missing Amazon purchases.
- Exact eBay and Amazon source links plus CSV export remain available for operator review.
- Profile 2 control can seed, start, resume, and read the audit without manual extension-page operation.

## Safety

- The workflow is read-only.
- It cannot place, cancel, refund, ship, edit, or delete an eBay or Amazon order.
- Different-recipient purchases are not called exact duplicates unless Amazon units exceed total eBay demand for that ASIN.
- Missing purchases remain provisional until every expected Amazon profile has been scanned.
