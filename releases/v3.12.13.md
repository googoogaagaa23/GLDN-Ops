# GLDN Ops v3.12.13

## What changed

- Existing Listings Policy Audit status can now be read exactly through the Profile 2 controller.
- The detailed policy-audit page can be opened directly by the local controller and updater service.
- Audit state, pending End review, and any prior End result are separate read-only fields.

## Safety

- Reading or opening the audit cannot edit or end an eBay listing.
- Needs review rows remain ineligible for ending.
- A reviewed Block row can reach only eBay's visible native review until a fresh exact `APPROVE END POLICY LISTINGS N` authorization is provided.
