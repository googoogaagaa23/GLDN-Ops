# GLDN Ops v3.12.60

Date: 2026-10-19

## Offer Insights Repairs

Offer readers are owned by the existing saved source/token/reader registry, not transient browser opener/window metadata. Switching or moving tabs within the same browser profile does not invalidate their identity. Wrong tokens, unrelated tabs, changed offers/buyers, wrong ASINs and normal/incognito crossings still stop reads. New readers follow the source's current window.

Valid sale and Amazon prices now produce a preliminary estimate even before Store plan and fee category are confirmed. Blank choices use explicitly labeled no Store/Starter and most-category assumptions. Confirmed choices override them, custom categories still require a rate, and invalid inputs never silently become defaults. Ads, tax, shipping and unknown costs remain disclosed.

The default general US schedule and per-order fees were checked against [eBay selling fees](https://www.ebay.com/help/selling/fees-credits-invoices/selling-fees?id=4822) and [Store selling fees](https://www.ebay.com/help/selling/fees-credits-invoices/store-selling-fees?id=4809). Category exceptions and listing-specific charges can change the result. This is an estimate, not guaranteed earnings.

The Amazon reader now prioritizes semantic primary purchase-price markers across core, apex and buy-box layouts, with legacy support. Unit, crossed-out list, subscription, used-offer and recommendation prices are excluded. Conflicting primary prices, selected subscriptions and different ASINs/variations remain errors rather than guessed costs.

An explicitly entered manual cost survives a failed refresh only for the same offer/ASIN and remains labeled Manual Amazon price. Automatically fetched prices are still cleared on failed refresh, and a mismatched item cannot retain a manual cost.
