## Checkout Item Repair

The affected M0/M8 Amazon review displayed dozens of product IDs for a checkout containing one motor. Those unrelated IDs caused the eBay SKU mismatch. Checkout IDs and titles now come from visible native shipment items, excluding recommendations, hidden rows and injected tools.

Current checkout IDs never fall back to older items. Cached amounts and dates require the same recent purchase ID; confirmation fallback is limited to the current tab. Copy stops when the page or items changed after Review. eBay normalizes duplicate IDs, uses the newest reviewed handoff and reports both sides of a true mismatch. Failed imports clear the approval checkbox.
