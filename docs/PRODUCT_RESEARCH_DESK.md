# GLDN Product Research Reference

## Current role

`extension/product-research-output.json` preserves the reviewed official-policy, Discord, Telegram, and historical Product Hunter research output for auditing and future rule refreshes. It is not displayed on the operator's Listing Policy Check page and it does not control which Product Hunter search words may be entered.

The operator workflow is deliberately simpler:

1. Open Product Hunter and enter any useful search words.
2. Product Hunter reads exact Amazon products and applies the current item-policy rules.
3. Paste Product Hunter output or raw Amazon links into **Listing Policy Check**.
4. Copy only Ready links into Bulk Poster; Review and Blocked items remain excluded.
5. Inspect the exact generated listing before any listing action.

## Research sources

The official refresh reviewed eBay's complete prohibited and restricted items hub and its 70 linked policies, plus supplemental intellectual-property guidance. Signed-in EcomSniper Discord and Telegram findings remain separate from official eBay evidence. Community findings can create Review only and cannot create Block.

The two GLDN operator rules intentionally block every pesticide and every spray can, even when eBay may allow a specific registered product or properly shipped aerosol under conditions.

Dropshipping-policy, fulfillment-source, and retail-arbitrage discussion remains excluded from this item-listing research set as requested.

## Refresh rules

1. Use official eBay pages for authoritative policy decisions.
2. Use only the approved signed-in Profile 2 interface for community research.
3. Preserve exact source URLs and record irrelevant or inconclusive findings as Ignore.
4. Publish community decisions as Review only.
5. Rebuild both the main GLDN Ops and standalone Product Hunter policy packs together.
6. Run focused and complete release checks before publishing updater metadata.

## Boundary

Research and keyword checks reduce known listing risk. They cannot guarantee authenticity, intellectual-property permission, regulatory compliance, recall status, eBay approval, or continued selling privileges, and they do not submit or modify listings.
